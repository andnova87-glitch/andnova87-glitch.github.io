NoteLab Travel Uploader Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

NoteLab Travel Uploader (internal name: travel-shorts, "the app") is a personal application used by a single person, its owner, on their own computer. It is not offered to other users. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API): the owner's own channel identity, the owner's uploaded videos and their status, and analytics for those videos (views, watch time, retention, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained). The app uploads videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's channel and updates the privacy status or scheduled publish time of those videos.
  • Meta / Instagram (Instagram Graph API with Facebook Login): the owner's Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account, the owner's media and its insights, public business account information and public hashtag media used for trend observation, and licensed audio search. The app publishes Reels to the owner's account.
  • TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API): the owner's basic TikTok profile (open ID, display name, avatar) and the creator information TikTok returns before a post (allowed privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video length). The app uploads the owner's own videos and photos to the owner's TikTok account, as drafts the owner finishes in the TikTok app or, where approved, as posts with the privacy level the owner chooses.

How the data is used

Only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos, to detect when the owner cancels a scheduled video, and to show the owner how their videos perform. No data is used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only in the operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager) on the owner's computer. Statistics are stored in a local database on the same computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, Meta's and TikTok's own APIs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google: remove "travel-shorts" at myaccount.google.com/permissions or via Google security settings.
  • Meta: remove the app under Facebook Settings → Business Integrations / Apps and Websites.
  • TikTok: remove the app in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions (wording may vary by app version).
  • Local data can be deleted by the owner at any time by removing the app's data folder and its credential-store entries. Stored API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days as required by the YouTube API Services policies.
