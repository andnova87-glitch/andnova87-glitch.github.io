travel-shorts

A personal tool for publishing my own travel short videos.

travel-shorts is a private, single-user application. It runs only on its owner's own computer and is not offered to the public. It edits the owner's own travel footage into short vertical videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram professional account. It also reads the performance statistics of those videos (views, watch time, likes) so that the owner can review which videos work best.

Google and Meta account access is used only for the owner's own accounts, only for the purposes above, and can be revoked at any time.
